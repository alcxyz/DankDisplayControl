## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-19

Not published yet; this section describes the initial widget as it stands.

- Initial DankMaterialShell bar widget for controller-first or couch-oriented Hyprland sessions: the bar pill shows the selected display policy, effective output count, and mirror state.
- The popout provides direct layout, mirror, and audio controls, laid out for use from a couch with large targets and minimal text.
- Development builds stamp an identifiable `X.Y.Z-dev.<commit>` version (`.dirty` for uncommitted changes) via `scripts/package.py` or the Nix `default.nix`.
